What is Circle Club?

Circle Club is a free online circle-drawing game hosted on ArcadeBloom. Draw one continuous loop around the center dot and get a score from 0 to 100. Play with a mouse, touch screen or keyboard, without an account or download.

How to draw a circle

  1. Choose Free draw to practise, or Daily challenge to match the dotted target circle.
  2. Press and hold on the canvas, then draw one loop around the center dot. Keep your distance from the dot steady and finish near your starting point.
  3. Release to see your score. Select Try again for another attempt, Copy link to challenge a friend, or Save score image to download your result.

Keyboard controls: Tab to the canvas and press Enter to start. Draw with the arrow keys; hold Shift for smaller steps. Press Enter again to score, or Escape to reset.

Circle Club questions

How is the circle score calculated?

The score measures how evenly your line stays from the center, how closely the ends meet, how fully it goes around the dot and how much extra path you draw. Daily mode also checks your circle against the target size. Very short strokes, tiny circles, incomplete loops and repeated loops may be rejected. A score of 100 is the maximum, not a ranking against other players.

How does the daily circle challenge work?

Daily challenge uses a target radius determined by the UTC calendar date. Everyone selecting the same date gets the same target size. The date is selected when you open the game or switch modes; reopen the mode after midnight UTC for the new day. A shared daily challenge link keeps its original date.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Draw with your finger on a touch screen or use a mouse on a computer. Keyboard controls are also available. The game needs JavaScript to draw and calculate scores.

Where are my scores saved?

Your latest thirty scores are saved in this browser on this device. There is no account or cloud score history. Clearing site data removes saved records. If browser storage is unavailable, you can still play. See ArcadeBloom’s privacy information.

What does a challenge link share?

After a valid drawing, Copy link includes your score and game mode in the URL, plus the date for a daily challenge. Your friend draws their own circle to try to beat that score. These URL scores are editable and are not verified leaderboard results.
